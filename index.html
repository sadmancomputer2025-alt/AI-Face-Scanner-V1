About AI Face Scanner

AI Face Scanner is a browser-based real-time face detection and analysis application.

Core Technologies

  • Runtime: Client-Side Modern JavaScript (ES6+)
  • AI Engine: face-api.js & TensorFlow.js-based browser AI
  • Hardware Acceleration: WebGL GPU Shaders
  • Video Feed: HTML5 MediaDevices API
Developed by Sadman Sakib

The application processes camera data locally in the browser with zero cloud dependencies for inference.

🧠

AI Technology & Models

AI Face Scanner harnesses four specialized neural network architectures executing directly on the client GPU:

Detector
Tiny Face Detector

A fast, performant MobileNetV1-based real-time face detector utilizing depthwise separable convolutions for high frame rates.

Geometry
Face Landmark 68

Locates 68 precision spatial landmark coordinates outlining jawline, brows, eyes, nose ridge, and lip contours.

Demographics
Age & Gender Model

Multi-task deep convolutional network simultaneously regressing estimated biological age and binary gender confidence.

Affective
Face Expression Model

Categorizes facial emotion softmax distributions across 7 universal states: happy, sad, angry, surprised, neutral, fearful, and disgusted.

🛡️

Privacy & Security

🔒 100% On-Device Client Processing

Your privacy is guaranteed by architectural design. AI Face Scanner does not connect to any recording, streaming, or logging servers.

  • Zero Network Uploads: Every frame analyzed from your webcam is held temporarily in browser memory (WebGL texture) and discarded immediately after inference.
  • No Storage: No photos, videos, or biometric signatures are written to localStorage, cookies, or remote databases.
  • Instant Deactivation: Clicking "Stop Camera" immediately terminates all MediaStream tracks and turns off hardware camera indicators.
